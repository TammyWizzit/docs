## How Wizzit Works

Getting started with Wizzit takes just a few minutes. Follow the steps below to go from sign-up to a fully connected workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [app.wizzit.com](https://app.wizzit.com) and verify your email address. Once confirmed, you'll land in your new workspace — ready to go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload or Create Your First Document">
    Use the dashboard to create a document from scratch or upload an existing file. Set its visibility to internal or client-facing and add tags to keep things organised from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Integrations or Use the SDK">
    Link Wizzit to the tools your team already relies on — or dive into the SDK to build custom workflows and automate document management programmatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Are you a developer looking to build on top of Wizzit? Head over to the [SDK section](/sdk/overview) for language-specific guides, code samples, and full reference documentation.
</Note>
