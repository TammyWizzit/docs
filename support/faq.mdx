This FAQ covers the questions our users ask most often. Whether you are just getting started, managing documents for your team, connecting third-party tools, or exploring the SDK, you will find quick answers here. If you cannot find what you are looking for, reach out to the support team at support@wizzit.com.

## Account & Getting Started

<Accordion title="How do I create a Wizzit account?">
  Visit [app.wizzit.com](https://app.wizzit.com) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your email address and choose a password, then verify your email using the link we send you. Your workspace is created automatically as soon as your email is confirmed — no extra setup required.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I invite team members to my workspace?">
  Yes. Go to **Settings → Members → Invite**, enter the email addresses of the people you want to add, and assign each one a role:

  - **Admin** — full access to workspace settings, billing, and members
  - **Editor** — can create, edit, and publish documents
  - **Viewer** — read-only access to documents shared with them

  Invitees receive an email with a link to join your workspace.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
  Yes. Wizzit offers a **14-day free trial** on all paid plans. You do not need to provide a credit card to start your trial. At the end of the trial period you can choose a plan or continue on the free Starter tier.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many workspaces can I have?">
  The number of workspaces depends on your plan:

  - **Starter** — one workspace per account
  - **Pro** — multiple workspaces supported
  - **Enterprise** — unlimited workspaces with centralised billing and administration

  You can upgrade your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing**.
</Accordion>

## Documents

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Internal and Client-Facing documents?">
  - **Internal documents** are only visible to members of your workspace. Use these for team runbooks, internal policies, and private knowledge bases.
  - **Client-Facing documents** can be shared with external clients via a shareable link or through the API. Access controls let you restrict who can view each document.

  You can switch a document between these two types at any time from the document's **Settings** panel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I import documents from other tools?">
  Yes. Wizzit supports importing documents from the following sources:

  - **Google Docs** — connect your Google account and select files to import
  - **Notion** — export pages from Notion and upload them directly
  - **Markdown files** — upload `.md` or `.mdx` files from your computer

  Start an import from **Settings → Import** and follow the on-screen steps.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a document size limit?">
  Individual document content can be up to **10 MB**. File attachments added to a document (images, PDFs, etc.) can each be up to **50 MB**. If you regularly work with very large files, contact the support team to discuss your requirements.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I version my documents?">
  Yes. Every time you publish a change to a document, Wizzit automatically creates a new version. You can review, compare, and restore any previous version by opening the document and clicking the **History** tab. Versions are retained for the lifetime of your workspace.
</Accordion>

## Integrations & API

<Accordion title="Where do I find my API key?">
  Go to **Settings → API Keys** and click **Create Key**. Give the key a descriptive name and select the scopes it needs, then click **Generate**.

  <Warning>
    Copy your API key immediately after creation — it is only shown once. If you lose it, you will need to revoke the old key and create a new one.
  </Warning>

  Store your key in a secrets manager or environment variable and never commit it to source control.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I delete an integration?">
  Deleting an integration immediately stops all future syncs between Wizzit and the connected service. Documents that were already synced before deletion are not affected — they remain in your workspace exactly as they were. If you want to resume syncing later, you will need to reconnect the integration from scratch.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many API calls can I make per minute?">
  The default rate limit is **100 requests per minute per API key**. If you exceed this limit, the API returns a `429 Too Many Requests` response. Implement exponential backoff in your client to handle this gracefully.

  <Tip>
    Enterprise plans can request higher rate limits. Contact your account manager or email support@wizzit.com with your use case.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Wizzit support webhooks?">
  Yes. You can register webhook endpoints in **Settings → Webhooks** or programmatically via the API. Wizzit sends a signed `POST` request to your endpoint whenever a subscribed event occurs (for example, a document being published or an integration syncing).

  See the [Webhooks documentation](/integrations/webhooks) for full details on event types, payload structure, and signature verification.
</Accordion>

## SDK

<Accordion title="Which programming languages does the SDK support?">
  The following SDKs are currently available:

  - **JavaScript / TypeScript** — install from npm as `@wizzit/sdk`
  - **Python** — install from PyPI as `wizzit`

  SDKs for **Ruby** and **Go** are in development and coming soon. If you need support for another language in the meantime, you can use the REST API directly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the SDK open source?">
  Yes. The Wizzit SDK is published under the **MIT licence** and the source code is publicly available.

  - **npm**: [`@wizzit/sdk`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@wizzit/sdk)
  - **PyPI**: [`wizzit`](https://pypi.org/project/wizzit/)

  Contributions, bug reports, and feature requests are welcome via the GitHub repository.
</Accordion>

## Billing & Plans

<Accordion title="What plans does Wizzit offer?">
  Wizzit has three plans to suit teams of every size:

  | Plan | Best for |
  |---|---|
  | **Starter** | Individuals and small teams getting started — free tier with core features |
  | **Pro** | Growing teams that need multiple workspaces and higher limits |
  | **Enterprise** | Large organisations requiring custom limits, SSO, and dedicated support |

  Visit the [pricing page](https://wizzit.com/pricing) for a full feature comparison and current pricing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change my plan at any time?">
  Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are billed a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
</Accordion>
